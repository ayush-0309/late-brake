The circuits

Six real circuits, laid out at true scale and run in their real direction. Read how each one works, then race it free in your browser.

  • Spielberg, Austria · 4.31 km Spielberg circuit A short, fast lap in the Styrian hills of Austria, first raced in 1969. Learn its three big stops, then race it free in your browser.
  • Monza, Italy · 5.79 km Monza circuit Italy's Temple of Speed, opened in 1922: long straights, three chicanes and the Parabolica. Learn the circuit, then race it free in your browser.
  • Silverstone, England · 5.88 km Silverstone circuit From wartime airfield to the home of the first world championship race in 1950. Learn its fast corners, then race it free in your browser.
  • Stavelot, Belgium · 6.98 km Spa-Francorchamps Seven kilometres through the Ardennes, with Eau Rouge, Pouhon and Blanchimont. Learn the longest circuit, then race it free in your browser.
  • Monte Carlo, Monaco · 3.33 km Monaco street circuit The streets of Monte Carlo, raced since 1929: barriers, the tunnel and the slowest hairpin of the year. Learn it, then race it free in your browser.
  • São Paulo, Brazil · 4.30 km Interlagos circuit São Paulo's circuit between two lakes, run anticlockwise since 1940 and home to famous title deciders. Learn it, then race it free in your browser.
Late Brake is an independent game and isn't affiliated with any racing series, team or circuit.
