How to play

Keyboard

  • ↑W throttle · ↓S brake
  • ←→ or AD steer
  • T reset to track · R restart · Esc pause
  • C camera: chase, cockpit or top-down
  • L racing line · G ghost · M sound

Gamepad & touch

  • Left stick steers, RT throttle, LT brake
  • Y resets to the track, Start pauses, Back restarts
  • On phones and tablets, steering buttons sit on the left and pedals on the right

Going fast

  • Stuck on a corner? Turn on the racing line with L: green is full throttle, yellow means lift, red means brake
  • The 150, 100 and 50 boards count down to every big stop
  • Brake in a straight line, then turn in as you come off the brakes
  • Leave the track for more than half a second and the lap won't count
