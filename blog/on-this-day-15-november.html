On this day: Berger's Adelaide win and Hamilton's seventh title

On 15 November, Berger led every lap in Adelaide in 1987, Rosberg won at Interlagos in 2015 and Hamilton took a seventh title in Turkey in 2020.

By Ayush Garg

A perfect Ferrari weekend, a fifth pole in a row and a record-equalling title: here is what happened in F1 on 15 November.

1987: Berger leads every lap in Adelaide

Ferrari's Gerhard Berger took pole for the Australian Grand Prix, the last race of the 1987 season, by 0.700 seconds from McLaren's Alain Prost.

He then led every lap and set the fastest lap, a 1:20.416. It was the only grand chelem of his career: pole, win, fastest lap and every lap led.

Ayrton Senna crossed the line second for Lotus but was disqualified for oversized brake ducts. That handed Ferrari a one-two, with Michele Alboreto second and Benetton's Thierry Boutsen third.

It was the last Ferrari win in Enzo Ferrari's lifetime. He died the following August.

2015: Rosberg wins again at Interlagos

With the titles already settled, Nico Rosberg took his fifth pole in a row at the Brazilian Grand Prix, 0.078 seconds ahead of his Mercedes team-mate Lewis Hamilton.

Rosberg won the race by 7.756 seconds from Hamilton, with Ferrari's Sebastian Vettel third. Hamilton had already clinched the 2015 drivers' title in Austin.

Williams' Felipe Massa was later disqualified after his right rear tyre was measured at 137°C, above the 110°C limit.

2020: Hamilton equals Schumacher with a seventh title

Racing Point's Lance Stroll took his first pole for the Turkish Grand Prix at Istanbul Park, where a freshly resurfaced track left the cars with very little grip in the wet.

Hamilton started sixth and won by 31.633 seconds from Sergio Pérez, with Vettel third.

The win gave Hamilton his seventh world title, equalling Michael Schumacher's record. It was also the seventh title in a row for a Mercedes driver.
