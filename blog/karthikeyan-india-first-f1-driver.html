How Narain Karthikeyan became India's first F1 driver

From Formula Maruti in India to a Jordan seat in 2005, Narain Karthikeyan's road to F1 ended in fourth place at the strangest race of the decade.

By Ayush Garg

In 2005 a driver from Coimbatore lined up on an F1 grid for the first time, and before the year was out he had scored points.

Narain Karthikeyan's route there took more than a decade, from Indian club racing to test days with three F1 teams.

From Formula Maruti to Europe

Karthikeyan was born in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, on 14 January 1977. At 15 he raced in Formula Maruti in India and in Formula Vauxhall Junior in Britain in the same year, 1993.

He won the British Formula Ford Winter Series in 1994 and the Formula Asia Championship in 1996.

On 14 June 2001 he became the first Indian to drive an F1 car, in a test for Jaguar at Silverstone. Tests for Jordan that year and Minardi in 2003 followed.

A Jordan seat in 2005

Jordan signed him for the 2005 season. On his debut at the Australian Grand Prix in Melbourne on 6 March, he qualified 12th and finished 15th, two laps down.

His first points came from an unlikely place.

Six cars at Indianapolis

At the United States Grand Prix on 19 June 2005, Ralf Schumacher crashed his Toyota at Turn 13 in practice after a left rear tyre failed.

Michelin said it could not guarantee its tyres were safe there for a full race. When no change to the track was agreed, the seven teams on Michelin tyres pulled their 14 cars into the pits after the formation lap.

Only the six cars on Bridgestone tyres raced: the two Ferraris, the two Jordans and the two Minardis. Michael Schumacher won for Ferrari ahead of Rubens Barrichello.

Karthikeyan's team-mate Tiago Monteiro was third. Karthikeyan finished fourth, a lap down, and scored five points.

They were his only F1 points, and they are still the only points scored by an Indian driver.

After Jordan

He became a test driver for Williams in 2006 and 2007, and raced for A1 Team India, giving the country its first A1 Grand Prix win at Zhuhai in December 2007.

Karthikeyan returned to F1 with HRT in 2011 and 2012, and raced at the first Indian Grand Prix at the Buddh International Circuit. He finished his F1 career with 46 starts, then raced on in Japan in Super Formula and Super GT.
