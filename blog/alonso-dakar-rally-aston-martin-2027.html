Alonso to race the 2027 Dakar Rally for Aston Martin

Two-time F1 champion Fernando Alonso will lead Aston Martin's first factory Dakar entry in January, alongside Sebastien Loeb and Lucas Moraes.

By Ayush Garg

Fernando Alonso will race in the Dakar Rally in January for Aston Martin, the team he drives for in F1.

The two-time world champion heads the brand's first factory entry in the event, alongside nine-time world rally champion Sebastien Loeb and Lucas Moraes.

A return after six years

Alonso first tackled the Dakar in 2020 with Toyota. He finished 13th overall and took second place on stage eight.

His navigator then, Marc Coma, will sit beside him again. Coma won the Dakar's motorcycle class five times.

The 2027 Dakar runs from 9 to 23 January in Portugal and Morocco, after the event moved away from Saudi Arabia.

The car and the team

The three drivers will race the Aston Martin Dreadnought, built and run by Prodrive, Aston Martin's engineering partner for the project.

The Dreadnought is the latest version of the car that raced as the Dacia Sandrider. Renault Group pulled out of cross-country rallying after Nasser Al-Attiyah won this year's Dakar in that car, and Prodrive then agreed the deal with Aston Martin.

Loeb, who has raced for Prodrive since 2021, is still chasing his first Dakar win. Moraes won the 2025 World Rally-Raid Championship with Toyota before moving to Dacia this year.

What it means for F1

Alonso, 45, extended his F1 contract with Aston Martin in September. He said he wanted to take on F1 and the Dakar in the same year, and stressed: "Formula 1 remains my priority."
