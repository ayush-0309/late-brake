Bearman to stay at Haas for 2027 as F1 grid is completed

Haas has confirmed Oliver Bearman for a third full F1 season in 2027, ending a long wait and filling the last open seat on next year's grid.

By Ayush Garg

Oliver Bearman will race for Haas again in 2027, the American team confirmed on Thursday at the start of the Qatar Grand Prix weekend.

The 21-year-old Briton's new deal fills the last vacant seat on the 2027 F1 grid.

A third full season

Next year will be Bearman's third full season with Haas. He made his F1 debut in 2024, standing in for Carlos Sainz at Ferrari in Saudi Arabia, then raced twice for Haas later that year before joining full time in 2025.

Team principal Ayao Komatsu called keeping Bearman a natural decision and said nobody was better placed to lead the team forward.

Bearman said he was delighted to stay and spoke of the team's ambitions: "we have unfinished business."

An end to the wait

The announcement came later than expected. At last weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, Bearman said he was disappointed that his contract still had not been settled so late in the season.

Bearman is a member of Ferrari's driver academy and wants to race for the Italian team in future. It was not made clear what had held up the deal.

An all-Ferrari academy line-up

Bearman's team-mate in 2027 will be Rafael Camara, the Brazilian who is also part of Ferrari's academy.

Camara joined Haas early after the team dropped Esteban Ocon during the Mexico City Grand Prix weekend, and he is racing for the team for the rest of 2026.

What happens next

Bearman and Camara are on track at Lusail for first practice at 7:00pm India time on Friday 27 November. The Qatar Grand Prix starts at 9:30pm India time on Sunday 29 November.
