McLaren to bring upgrades for 2027 to F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix

McLaren will run new parts on the MCL40 in Las Vegas that it says lay the groundwork for its 2027 car, at a track where it has struggled.

By Ayush Garg

McLaren will bring upgrades to its MCL40 at next weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, a race after it introduced its 2027 front wing concept in Brazil.

Team principal Andrea Stella said the new parts form "the foundation" for next year's car, adding to the updates McLaren has already run.

Building towards 2027

Stella said the front wing that appeared at Interlagos made the car more driveable and predictable. The Las Vegas package is meant to build on that.

He also looked back at the team's form since its summer upgrades. McLaren was in a position to win after the Hungary update, Stella said, and should have won in Madrid before a Virtual Safety Car hurt Lando Norris.

Rivals then caught up with their own upgrades, he said, and McLaren paid for a run of poor qualifying sessions.

Norris's wet win at Interlagos last weekend, from pole, ended five rounds without a McLaren podium. That was the team's worst run since the first half of 2023.

A track McLaren has never mastered

Las Vegas has gone badly for McLaren in each of its three races there since F1 returned in 2023.

  • 2023: Both cars went out in the first part of qualifying. Norris crashed heavily on lap three, and Oscar Piastri scored a single point.
  • 2024: Norris and Piastri qualified sixth and eighth and finished sixth and seventh, 27 seconds behind the top five.
  • 2025: Norris took pole, then finished second with Piastri fourth behind Max Verstappen. Both cars were disqualified because their rearmost skid blocks, the wear strips under the floor, were too worn.

Stella expects the long straights to test McLaren again. The team has struggled where top speed counts, as at Monza and Baku, which he linked partly to its shorter gear ratios.

Even so, he said the team goes to Las Vegas with some optimism because the car is quicker than before.

When to watch in India

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is round 21 of 23. The race starts at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November (8pm on Saturday in Las Vegas).

First practice starts at 6am India time on Friday 20 November, and qualifying at 9:30am India time on Saturday 21 November.
