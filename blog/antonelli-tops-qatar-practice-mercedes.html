Antonelli fastest in both F1 Qatar GP practice sessions

Kimi Antonelli led a Mercedes one-two in both Friday practice sessions at Lusail, with a second session stopped twice by red flags.

By Ayush Garg

Kimi Antonelli topped both practice sessions for the F1 Qatar Grand Prix on Friday, leading Mercedes team-mate George Russell each time at the Lusail International Circuit.

The new world champion set a best lap of 1m23.077s on medium tyres in the second session, 0.127 seconds quicker than Russell.

Mercedes clear of McLaren and Red Bull

McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri were third and fourth in second practice, just under half a second behind Antonelli. McLaren ran an older version of its floor on Friday.

Red Bull's Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar were fifth and sixth, about half a second back. Ferrari had a slow day: Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton were seventh and eighth, more than a second off the pace.

Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad completed the top 10.

The first session followed the same pattern. Antonelli led a Mercedes one-two there too, with Hadjar third ahead of Norris, Piastri and Verstappen.

Two red flags

Second practice was stopped twice. The first red flag came around halfway through, so marshals could clear gravel that cars had dragged onto the track after running wide, including Leclerc after a big spin at Turn 7.

Alpine's Pierre Gasly also ended up stuck in the gravel at Turn 14. Race control added 13 minutes to the session to make up for the lost running.

Late on, Hamilton's Ferrari appeared to lose power and stopped on track, bringing out a virtual safety car (a period when every driver must slow to a set speed).

Earlier, Hamilton had repassed Verstappen on the main straight and moved towards the Red Bull. Race control noted it but decided no investigation was needed.

What happens next

Third practice starts at 8:00pm India time on Saturday 28 November, with qualifying at 11:30pm.

The race, over 57 laps, starts at 9:30pm India time on Sunday 29 November. It is the penultimate round of the season, with Antonelli and Mercedes already crowned champions.
