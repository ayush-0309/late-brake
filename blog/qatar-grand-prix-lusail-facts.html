Six F1 Qatar Grand Prix facts that sound made up

A stand-in first race, a year off for the football World Cup and a title won on a Saturday: six true stories from F1's visits to Lusail.

By Ayush Garg

With F1 heading to Lusail for the Qatar Grand Prix this weekend, here are six true stories from the circuit.

1. The first race was a stand-in

Qatar's first Grand Prix, on 21 November 2021, took the slot of the Australian Grand Prix, which was cancelled because of COVID-19.

Lewis Hamilton won it for Mercedes.

2. The track was built in under a year

Lusail International Circuit opened in 2004. It was built in just under a year by 1,000 workers, at a cost of US$58 million.

F1 did not race there until 17 years later.

3. Motorbikes raced under its lights first

Permanent floodlights went up in 2007. The first night race for MotoGP, the motorcycle world championship, followed at Lusail in March 2008.

When F1 arrived in 2021, it became the fourth full night race on the calendar, after Singapore, Bahrain and Sakhir.

4. It took a year off for the football World Cup

There was no Qatar Grand Prix in 2022 because the country hosted the FIFA World Cup.

F1 came back in 2023 under a 10-year contract. Before that return, the circuit was renovated and its capacity rose from 8,000 to 52,000.

5. A title was won on a Saturday

Max Verstappen clinched his third consecutive title in the sprint on Saturday 7 October 2023, before the Grand Prix had started.

He finished second, 1.871 seconds behind Oscar Piastri, who took his first sprint win. Sergio Perez crashed out of the sprint, which left Verstappen out of reach.

6. The 2023 race was rationed by the lap

Because of tyre safety concerns, the FIA limited every set to 18 laps in the 2023 race. It was the first time it had enforced stint lengths in an F1 race for safety reasons.

In extreme heat, several drivers called it the hardest race they had ever driven. Logan Sargeant retired with heat stroke and dehydration, and Esteban Ocon was sick in his car twice but still finished seventh. Pirelli has set a 27-lap limit for this year's race.
