On this day: Vettel's record eighth win and Sainz's first podium

On 17 November, Sebastian Vettel set a record run of wins in Austin in 2013 and Carlos Sainz took his first podium from last at Interlagos in 2019.

By Ayush Garg

Two races run on 17 November ended with a record and a pair of first podiums.

2013: Vettel wins an eighth race in a row

Sebastian Vettel won the United States Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas in Austin from pole position, his eighth win in a row. It was a new F1 record for consecutive wins within a single season.

The Red Bull driver also set the fastest lap, a 1:39.856, and crossed the line 6.284 seconds clear of Lotus's Romain Grosjean. His team-mate Mark Webber finished third, 8.396 seconds behind.

Vettel had already sealed his fourth title in a row three weeks earlier, by winning the Indian Grand Prix at the Buddh International Circuit. After Austin he led the standings with 372 points, 145 ahead of Fernando Alonso.

2019: Sainz goes from last to the podium at Interlagos

Max Verstappen won the Brazilian Grand Prix from pole for Red Bull, but the drama came behind him.

Pierre Gasly finished second for Toro Rosso, 6.077 seconds back, for his first F1 podium. Lewis Hamilton crossed the line third, but a five-second penalty for hitting Alexander Albon's Red Bull dropped him to seventh.

That promoted Carlos Sainz to third. The McLaren driver had started 20th, last on the grid, after failing to set a time in qualifying. It was his first podium and McLaren's first since the 2014 Australian Grand Prix.

Ferrari had a race to forget. Charles Leclerc and Sebastian Vettel collided on the back straight on lap 66, and both retired.

The podium of Verstappen, Gasly and Sainz was the youngest in F1 history at the time, beating the record set at the 2008 Italian Grand Prix.
