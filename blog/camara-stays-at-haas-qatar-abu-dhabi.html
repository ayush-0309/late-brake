Camara to finish F1 season with Haas, giving up F2 title bid

Haas will keep Rafael Camara for Qatar and Abu Dhabi, ending the Brazilian's chance to win the Formula 2 title he currently leads by seven points.

By Ayush Garg

Haas will keep Rafael Camara in its car for the last two F1 rounds of 2026, the Qatar and Abu Dhabi Grands Prix.

Because drivers cannot race in F1 and Formula 2 on the same weekend, the decision ends the 21-year-old Brazilian's bid for the F2 title, which he leads by seven points.

Moved up early

Haas confirmed Camara in October as Esteban Ocon's full-time replacement for 2027. He was brought into the seat early after Ocon's relationship with the team broke down.

Test driver Ryo Hirakawa took over Ocon's car part of the way through the Mexico City Grand Prix weekend, before Camara made his F1 debut at his home race in Brazil.

He finished 20th in a wet race at Interlagos and 16th in Las Vegas on Saturday. Haas chose to keep him in the car rather than bring Hirakawa back, so he gains more experience before his first full season.

Team principal Ayao Komatsu said: "We're excited to be able to finish the season with Rafa driving."

The F2 title fight opens up

Camara's absence leaves the Formula 2 championship to his rivals. Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov is second, seven points behind, and Alex Dunne is third, 18 points back.

What happens next

The Qatar Grand Prix weekend runs from 27 to 29 November at Lusail, and the season ends in Abu Dhabi from 4 to 6 December.
