Williams to race one-off chrome livery at F1 Las Vegas GP

Williams will run a special chrome livery on its FW48s at this weekend's F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, designed with title partner Atlassian.

By Ayush Garg

Williams will race a one-off chrome livery at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, designed to shine under the lights of the Strip.

Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz will drive the FW48s in the new colours, and both will wear race suits and gloves to match.

Built with Atlassian

The team created the design with Atlassian, the software company that is its title partner. Motorsport.com describes the finish as blue chrome.

Team principal James Vowles linked the look to the team's history, which stretches back almost 50 years. He said that history was "fuel for our future" and that the livery reflects it.

Atlassian's co-founder and chief executive, Mike Cannon-Brookes, said the car "looks great in Las Vegas".

Why Las Vegas

Motorsport.com reports that several teams are changing their liveries for the race, because Las Vegas has become one of the biggest commercial weekends of the year for teams and their partners.

It is the third and last of the season's three races in the United States.

A tough season to brighten

Williams has had a hard first year under the new 2026 rules. It sits ninth of 11 teams in the constructors' championship and has already switched its development work to next year's car.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix starts at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November. First practice begins at 6:00am India time on Friday.
