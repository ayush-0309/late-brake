Bottas keeps his Cadillac F1 seat for 2027 ahead of Herta

Cadillac will keep Valtteri Bottas alongside Sergio Perez in 2027, choosing him over test driver Colton Herta and completing next year's F1 grid.

By Ayush Garg

Cadillac has confirmed that Valtteri Bottas will stay with its F1 team in 2027, alongside Sergio Perez.

The decision ends weeks of talk that American test driver Colton Herta would take his seat, and it completes the 22-car grid for next season. Cadillac joined this year as F1's 11th team.

Herta has to wait

Herta, a 26-year-old Californian who raced in IndyCar, had been working towards the 40 points a driver needs for an FIA superlicence, the permit required to race in F1. He reached that mark with practice outings, including one at Interlagos earlier this month.

Cadillac weighed promoting him, but the team's owners chose to stay with the line-up that started its first season. The team said it will name its reserve and test drivers for 2027 later, so Herta's role is not yet known.

Experience over youth

Bottas is 37 and has made 265 Grand Prix starts, with 10 wins.

His opening message after the announcement was short: "To whom it may concern, I'm staying!"

Cadillac said the decision gives the team continuity and stability as it builds on its first year in F1. Dan Towriss, chief executive of majority owner TWG Motorsports, praised the Finn's experience and commitment.

What happens next

Bottas and Perez go straight into this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, the first of the last three races of 2026. First practice starts at 6:00am India time on Friday 20 November, and the race at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November.
