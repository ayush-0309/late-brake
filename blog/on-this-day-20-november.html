On this day: Moss wins at Riverside and Vettel bows out in Abu Dhabi

On 20 November Stirling Moss won the 1960 United States Grand Prix from pole, and in 2022 Sebastian Vettel scored a point in his final F1 race.

By Ayush Garg

Two season finales fell on 20 November: one in California in 1960 and one in Abu Dhabi in 2022.

1960: Moss wins the season finale at Riverside

Stirling Moss won the United States Grand Prix at Riverside International Raceway in California, the last of the 10 races of 1960. He took pole with a lap of 1:54.4 in a Lotus.

Moss covered the 75 laps of the 5.271 kilometre road course in 2 hours 28 minutes 52.2 seconds. Innes Ireland finished 38.0 seconds behind, also in a Lotus, with Bruce McLaren third for Cooper.

Jack Brabham came fourth, a lap down, and set the fastest lap. He finished the year as world champion for the second season in a row, on 43 points.

It was the last race of F1's 2.5 litre engine rules. About 25,000 people watched, and Moss collected a cheque for $7,500 from the promoter, Alec Ulmann.

2022: Vettel's last race as Verstappen wins again

Max Verstappen won the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix from pole, 8.771 seconds ahead of Ferrari's Charles Leclerc. It was his 15th win of the season, a record.

Sergio Pérez finished third, 10.093 seconds behind. That left Leclerc second in the championship on 308 points, three ahead of Pérez.

Sebastian Vettel, the four-time champion, finished 10th in his final F1 race. It was his 300th Grand Prix entry, and he signed off with one more point for Aston Martin.

The race was also the last for Daniel Ricciardo at McLaren, who finished ninth, and for Nicholas Latifi at Williams and Mick Schumacher at Haas.
