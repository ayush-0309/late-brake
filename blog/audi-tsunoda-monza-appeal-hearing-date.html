FIA court to hear Audi's Tsunoda Monza appeal on 25 November

The FIA's International Court of Appeal will rule on Audi's challenge to Yuki Tsunoda's Italian Grand Prix point at a closed hearing in Paris.

By Ayush Garg

The FIA has set 25 November as the date its International Court of Appeal will hear Audi's challenge over Yuki Tsunoda's result at the Italian Grand Prix.

The case could hand Audi's Gabriel Bortoleto the last point at Monza, more than two months after the race.

An extra formation lap

The race on 6 September was stopped with a red flag at the end of lap 3 so a barrier could be repaired, then restarted from the grid.

At the end of the formation lap before the restart, Tsunoda, driving for Racing Bulls, stopped inside his grid box but with his car at an angle. The race director then ordered another formation lap.

The rules say a driver who causes an extra formation lap must start from the end of the pit lane. The stewards looked into it and took no further action.

According to The Race, they accepted Racing Bulls' case that Tsunoda stayed in his box, held up no one and that the extra lap was the race director's own call.

Why Audi appealed

Tsunoda finished 10th and scored one point. Bortoleto was 11th, one place behind.

Audi lodged its appeal on 9 September, arguing that Tsunoda did cause the extra lap and should have been penalised. A penalty could push him out of the points and move Bortoleto up to tenth.

What happens next

The hearing starts at 09:00 Paris time on Wednesday 25 November (13:30 India time) at the FIA's offices on the Place de la Concorde.

At the FIA's request it will be closed to the public and the media. The FIA says the decision will be published as soon as possible afterwards.

The date falls between the last three rounds of the season: Las Vegas this coming week, then Qatar and the Abu Dhabi finale.
