Mercedes to give only Antonelli new turbo at F1 Las Vegas GP

Mercedes will run its upgraded ADUO turbo on Kimi Antonelli's car in Las Vegas, but not on George Russell's, to protect his grid position.

By Ayush Garg

Mercedes will introduce an upgraded turbo on new world champion Kimi Antonelli's car at this weekend's F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix.

Team-mate George Russell will not get the part, because fitting it to his car would bring a grid penalty.

An upgrade earned under ADUO

The upgrade comes through ADUO, short for Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities. It is F1's system for letting engine makers who fall behind the best engine make extra upgrades.

Mercedes was judged to be more than 2% behind the benchmark, Red Bull's engine, which earned it the extra upgrade this year.

"We will have the ADUO enabled turbo specification available from this weekend," team principal Toto Wolff said in the team's race preview.

He said the main aim was to prove the part on a race weekend, and that the gain in performance is expected to be small.

Why Russell misses out

Russell has already used more engine parts than the rules allow this season, after a run of reliability problems. Taking another turbo would trigger an automatic grid penalty.

Wolff said the team's priority with Russell is protecting his grid position, because the gain is probably too small to be worth a penalty.

Russell is third in the drivers' championship, 17 points behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen in the fight for second. Wolff named helping him finish as high as possible as one of the team's goals for the last three races.

What happens next

Mercedes has already won both titles this year, so the run is mainly about learning for 2027. First practice in Las Vegas starts at 6:00am India time on Friday 20 November, and the race at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November.
