On this day: Webber wins at Interlagos and Rosberg takes the title

Mark Webber won Rubens Barrichello's last F1 race at Interlagos in 2011, and five years later Nico Rosberg became champion in Abu Dhabi.

By Ayush Garg

Two season finales fell on 27 November: one was a farewell at Interlagos, the other settled a title between team-mates.

2011: Webber wins as Barrichello bows out

Mark Webber won the 2011 Brazilian Grand Prix at Interlagos, his only win of the season. His Red Bull team-mate Sebastian Vettel finished second, giving the team its third one-two of the year.

Vettel had started from pole, his 15th of the season, which broke Nigel Mansell's 1992 record for the most poles in one year. He led early on but let Webber through when his gearbox began to give trouble.

McLaren's Jenson Button finished third after repassing Ferrari's Fernando Alonso late in the race.

It was the last F1 race for Rubens Barrichello, the 11-time Grand Prix winner and, at that point, the most experienced driver in the sport's history. It was also the final race for Jarno Trulli.

2016: Rosberg becomes champion in Abu Dhabi

Nico Rosberg arrived at the 2016 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix 12 points ahead of his Mercedes team-mate Lewis Hamilton. A podium finish would make him champion.

Hamilton started from pole and led. In the closing laps he slowed the pace, keeping Rosberg close behind him so that the chasing cars could catch up. The Mercedes pit wall told him several times to speed up, but he did not.

Ferrari's Sebastian Vettel got within a second of Rosberg, with Red Bull's Max Verstappen just behind, but Rosberg held on. Hamilton won the race, Rosberg finished second and Vettel was third.

It was Rosberg's only world title. A week later he announced his retirement from F1, making Abu Dhabi his last race.
