Gasly says cooling problem cost him an F1 Las Vegas podium

Pierre Gasly finished fifth in Las Vegas but says his Alpine ran with reduced power for 45 laps because the car was overheating.

By Ayush Garg

Pierre Gasly says a cooling problem on his Alpine stopped him fighting for a podium, or even the win, at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The Frenchman still finished fifth, 2.927 seconds behind winner Max Verstappen, and called it Alpine's best race as a team.

Power turned down

Gasly started eighth and ran as high as third, but around the time he reached that position his team told him over the radio that the car was overheating.

To keep the power unit's temperatures under control, Alpine had him use reduced engine modes. He said the team turned the power down for 45 laps, and that in the slowest mode he was losing more than a second a lap.

"I really feel we had the podium," Gasly told Motorsport.com, adding that he kept asking to go back to full power. He said the team did not see the problem coming.

Both cars in the top six

Gasly fell back down the order while his power was reduced, then gained places late on as George Russell crashed out of contention and Ferrari's Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton collided.

Team-mate Franco Colapinto finished sixth, 7.955 seconds behind Verstappen, so both Alpines scored. Gasly's 10 points take him to 52 and keep him 10th in the drivers' standings. Colapinto is 12th on 35.

What happens next

The next round is the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail International Circuit, which starts at 9:30pm India time on Sunday 29 November. The season ends in Abu Dhabi a week later.
