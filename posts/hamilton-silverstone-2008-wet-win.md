Lewis Hamilton arrived at Silverstone in July 2008 under pressure. He left with one of F1's finest wet-weather wins and a share of the championship lead.

## Two bad races

The British Grand Prix on 6 July was round 9 of 18. In Canada, Hamilton had crashed into Kimi Räikkönen's Ferrari, which was waiting at a red light at the end of the pit lane.

That earned him a 10-place grid penalty for the French Grand Prix. There he took a drive-through penalty for passing Sebastian Vettel by cutting a chicane, and again failed to score.

Ferrari's Felipe Massa led the standings on 48 points. BMW Sauber's Robert Kubica had 46, Räikkönen 43 and Hamilton 38.

## Rain at the start

Hamilton's McLaren team-mate Heikki Kovalainen took pole with 1:21.049. Mark Webber's Red Bull was second, Räikkönen third and Hamilton fourth. Massa started ninth.

It rained before the 60-lap race and every driver started on intermediate tyres, the ones made for a damp track. Hamilton passed Webber and Räikkönen at the first corner to run second.

Webber spun on the first lap and fell from fourth to last. Massa spun on laps 1 and 3, and spun again later in the race.

On lap 5, Hamilton passed Kovalainen into Stowe corner and took the lead.

## Nobody could stay with him

As others slid off, Hamilton stayed on the road. McLaren gave him fresh intermediates at his stops on about lap 21 and on lap 38.

Kovalainen and Räikkönen both spun. Honda's Rubens Barrichello gambled on full wet tyres and was briefly around nine seconds a lap faster than most of the field, climbing to second before he had to stop again.

Hamilton won in 1:39:09.440. BMW Sauber's Nick Heidfeld was second, 1 minute 8.577 seconds behind, and Barrichello third, 1 minute 22.273 seconds back. Räikkönen and Kovalainen finished a lap down.

Only 13 of the 20 starters finished. Massa was last of them, two laps behind.

"I couldn't see anything," Hamilton said afterwards. It was the biggest winning margin in F1 since the 1995 Australian Grand Prix.

## Level at the top

The win put Hamilton, Massa and Räikkönen level on 48 points at the halfway mark of the season.

The fight went all the way to the final race of the season, where Hamilton beat Massa to the title by a single point.
