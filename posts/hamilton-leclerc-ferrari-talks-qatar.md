Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc say their talks since colliding at the Las Vegas Grand Prix have been positive, as Ferrari arrives in Qatar trying to end a run of clashes between its drivers.

Both spoke at Lusail on Thursday but would not say what the team has agreed, keeping the details inside Ferrari.

## What happened in Las Vegas

The Ferrari drivers were fighting over sixth place when Leclerc spun Hamilton around. Leclerc was given a 10-second penalty and finished 10th, while Hamilton recovered to seventh.

It came weeks after the pair collided at the Mexico City Grand Prix. Team principal Fred Vasseur called the Las Vegas incident unacceptable.

## What the drivers said

Hamilton said the discussions had been good but would stay internal. "We're going to figure it out," he said, adding that these were very challenging times for Vasseur, Leclerc and himself.

Leclerc said he took responsibility for the Las Vegas collision and would rather discuss any solution inside the team. He also said his contract contains no clause making him Ferrari's number one driver, and that he was hurt by people questioning his commitment to the team.

## What's at stake

Ferrari is second in the constructors' championship, 77 points ahead of Red Bull, with two rounds left.

First practice in Qatar starts at 7:00pm India time on Friday 27 November, and the race begins at 9:30pm India time on Sunday 29 November.
