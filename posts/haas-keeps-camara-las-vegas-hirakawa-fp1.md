Haas has confirmed Rafael Camara will race for the team again at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, his second F1 weekend after his debut in Brazil.

Ryo Hirakawa, who stood in for the dropped Esteban Ocon in Mexico, returns in Ollie Bearman's car for first practice on Friday.

## A second chance after a wet debut

Camara, 21, made his F1 debut at his home race in São Paulo in changeable weather. He crashed in the second part of qualifying, qualified 13th and started 11th after grid penalties for other drivers.

A difficult stint on intermediate tyres, the grooved tyres used on a damp track, then dropped him towards the back of the field.

Haas had already named the Brazilian as one of its drivers for 2027. He leads the Formula 2 standings, and Autosport reports that his plans for the last two rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi are not settled.

Team principal Ayao Komatsu said Camara "showed us and the world why we signed him for 2027" in Brazil.

## Hirakawa back in the car

Hirakawa was called up on the Saturday morning of the Mexico City Grand Prix after Haas stood Ocon down. He finished 10th on his first F1 start and scored a point after post-race penalties for other drivers.

He missed Brazil to race in the World Endurance Championship finale, where he was crowned 2026 champion with Toyota. Las Vegas will be his fourth FP1 outing for Haas this year.

## What happens next

The Las Vegas weekend opens the last triple header of the season, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi. First practice starts at 6:00am India time on Friday 20 November, and the race at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November.

Haas has not yet named its line-up for the final two races.
