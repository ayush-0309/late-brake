Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix for Red Bull, finishing 0.639 seconds ahead of McLaren's Lando Norris after 50 laps.

Oscar Piastri was third, 1.529 seconds behind, to give McLaren a double podium in a race where the top five were split by less than three seconds.

## A podium from 15th

Verstappen started from pole after world champion Kimi Antonelli took a five-place grid penalty for exceeding his engine allocation.

Norris started third, dropped as low as 11th and fought back to second. Piastri started 15th and climbed to third.

Antonelli finished fourth for Mercedes, 2.574 seconds behind the winner, and held off Alpine's Pierre Gasly by 0.353 seconds. Franco Colapinto made it two Alpines in the top six.

## Russell hits the wall

George Russell was chasing Antonelli for second when he locked up and hit the wall with three laps to go.

He kept going but fell to ninth, 13.192 seconds behind Verstappen.

An earlier safety car came out after Sergio Perez and Rafael Camara collided, and most of the field used it to make their pit stops. Williams' Alex Albon did not start after a brake overheating problem.

## Ferrari clash again

Ferrari's drivers collided while fighting over sixth place. Charles Leclerc spun Lewis Hamilton around and received a 10-second penalty, which dropped him to 10th.

Hamilton, who had already served a 10-second penalty for leaving the track to pass Norris, recovered to seventh. Team principal Fred Vasseur called the collision "unacceptable".

## What happens next

Two rounds remain. The Qatar Grand Prix weekend at Lusail runs from 27 to 29 November, and the season ends in Abu Dhabi from 4 to 6 December.
