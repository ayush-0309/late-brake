George Russell topped second practice for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, setting a 1:47.272 for Mercedes as the chequered flag fell on a slowly drying track.

Alpine's Pierre Gasly was second, 0.071 seconds behind, after a late lap of his own. Heavy rain earlier meant nobody set a lap time at all in first practice.

## A first session with no times

Rain fell before the first session on Thursday evening in Las Vegas, and the track was full of standing water. Some drivers went out on full wet tyres, but nobody tried a flying lap.

Teams chose to save their limited supply of wet tyres. Drivers reported aquaplaning, where a tyre rides up on the water and loses grip, on the low-grip street surface.

## Late laps decide second practice

The second hour started on wet tyres before the field switched to intermediates, the tyres for a damp track. Air temperatures dropped as low as 11C.

Ferrari's Charles Leclerc led for much of the session and finished third, 0.333 seconds off Russell. Red Bull's Isack Hadjar was fourth after Haas driver Oliver Bearman held him up on his final lap.

Oscar Piastri was fifth for McLaren, ahead of Lewis Hamilton, Lando Norris and the new champion, Kimi Antonelli. Franco Colapinto and Fernando Alonso completed the top 10.

Max Verstappen spent much of the hour in the garage, complained of a lack of power and finished 13th. A plastic bag blowing onto the track caused a brief red flag near the end.

Russell played down the result. "Realistically we were not the fastest today," he told the BBC, pointing to the pace of McLaren and Leclerc.

## What happens next

The forecast is dry for the rest of the weekend, so teams start again on slick tyres with little data. Third practice runs at 6:00am India time on Saturday 21 November, and qualifying at 9:30am.

The race starts at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November. Russell is fighting Verstappen for second place in the drivers' championship over the last three races.
