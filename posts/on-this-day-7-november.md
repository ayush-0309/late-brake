A great rivalry's final race, a team's first championship and a home podium in Mexico all fall on 7 November.

## 1993: Senna wins his last Grand Prix

Ayrton Senna won the 1993 Australian Grand Prix in Adelaide for McLaren, from pole position. It was the 41st and last win of his F1 career.

Alain Prost finished second for Williams, 9.259 seconds behind, with his team-mate Damon Hill third. It was Prost's final Grand Prix: he retired as the 1993 world champion, his fourth title.

The race was also Senna's last for McLaren before his move to Williams, and Riccardo Patrese's 256th and final F1 start.

## 2010: Red Bull's first constructors' title

Sebastian Vettel led a Red Bull one-two in the Brazilian Grand Prix at Interlagos, 4.243 seconds ahead of Mark Webber. Ferrari's Fernando Alonso was third.

The result gave Red Bull its first constructors' championship, with 469 points to McLaren's 421. Nico Hülkenberg had started from pole for Williams, the first of his career.

With one race left, Alonso led the drivers' standings on 246 points, ahead of Webber on 238 and Vettel on 231. Vettel won the title a week later in Abu Dhabi.

## 2021: Verstappen wins in Mexico City

Max Verstappen won the 2021 Mexico City Grand Prix for Red Bull. He started third behind the Mercedes pair of Valtteri Bottas and Lewis Hamilton, and led by the first corner.

Hamilton finished second, 16.555 seconds behind. Sergio Pérez was third, the first Mexican driver to finish on the podium at his home race.

The win stretched Verstappen's championship lead over Hamilton to 19 points.
