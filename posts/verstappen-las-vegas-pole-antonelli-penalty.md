Max Verstappen will start the Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole position after Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli, the fastest man in qualifying, took a five-place grid penalty.

Antonelli set a 1:33.893 to beat Verstappen's Red Bull by 0.258 seconds. The new world champion's penalty, for using more engine parts than the rules allow, drops him to sixth on the grid.

## A lap built on the tow

Antonelli was the last driver to start a final lap, with a second to spare before the chequered flag and only one second behind Racing Bulls' Arvid Lindblad. On the Strip's long straights, that slipstream, or "tow", from the car ahead was worth a lot of time.

"The tow is so powerful here," Antonelli told the BBC. It was his eighth fastest qualifying lap of 2026 and his first since Austin.

George Russell was third fastest, 0.073 seconds behind Verstappen, and starts second. Lando Norris was only 0.020 seconds slower than Russell and lines up third for McLaren.

Isack Hadjar starts fourth in the second Red Bull, ahead of Lindblad, Antonelli, Liam Lawson and the Alpines of Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto.

## Both Ferraris out in Q2

Ferrari failed to get a car into the top-10 shootout for the first time since Imola last year. Charles Leclerc locked up at Turn 7 on his key lap and qualified 11th, with Lewis Hamilton 12th.

Hamilton said damage to his car's floor and a problem with energy deployment had cost him time. Both Ferraris move up a place on the grid because of other penalties.

Five drivers have grid penalties for power unit changes. Oscar Piastri qualified seventh but drops 10 places. Carlos Sainz and Lance Stroll also drop 10 places, and Oliver Bearman 35.

## What happens next

The 50-lap race starts at 9:30am India time today, Sunday 22 November (8pm on Saturday in Las Vegas).

Antonelli and Mercedes have already won both titles. Verstappen is second in the drivers' standings on 279 points, 17 ahead of Russell, so the front row is also a fight for runner-up.

## Race it on Late Brake

Las Vegas isn't in Late Brake, but Monza, where the photo above was taken, is one of six real circuits in the game. Its long straights reward a good exit from every corner: set a lap, then race your own ghost and chase the gold medal time.
