A first Grand Prix in India, a podium decided after the race and a new record in Mexico City. Here is what happened in Formula 1 on 30 October.

## 2011: Vettel wins the first Indian Grand Prix

Formula 1 raced in India for the first time at the Buddh International Circuit, near Delhi. Sebastian Vettel won for Red Bull, 8.433 seconds ahead of McLaren's Jenson Button.

Fernando Alonso finished third for Ferrari. Vettel took pole, led all 60 laps and set the fastest lap, the first grand slam of his career.

Narain Karthikeyan raced at home for HRT and finished 18th. Karun Chandhok drove in first practice on Friday but did not race.

Lewis Hamilton and Felipe Massa collided at turn five. Massa was given a drive-through penalty, and Hamilton pitted for a new front wing and finished seventh.

## 2016: Ricciardo gets third after the race

Lewis Hamilton won the Mexican Grand Prix from his Mercedes team-mate Nico Rosberg, cutting Rosberg's championship lead from 26 points to 19.

The fight for third was decided later. Max Verstappen crossed the line ahead of Sebastian Vettel but was given a five-second penalty for cutting a corner to stay in front.

Vettel stood on the podium, then received a ten-second penalty for moving under braking while defending from Daniel Ricciardo. Ricciardo was promoted to third, Verstappen dropped to fourth and Vettel to fifth.

## 2022: Verstappen breaks the record for wins in a season

Six years later, Verstappen won the Mexico City Grand Prix for Red Bull, 15.186 seconds ahead of Lewis Hamilton's Mercedes.

It was his 14th win of 2022, one more than the previous record of 13 shared by Michael Schumacher (2004) and Sebastian Vettel (2013). He finished that season with 15.

It was also his fourth win in Mexico, one more than Jim Clark. Sergio Perez finished third at his home race, 18.097 seconds behind.
