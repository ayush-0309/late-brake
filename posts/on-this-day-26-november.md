Two season finales at Abu Dhabi's Yas Marina Circuit fell on 26 November: one said goodbye to a Ferrari favourite, the other closed a record-breaking season.

## 2017: Bottas wins as Massa bows out

Valtteri Bottas won the 2017 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix from pole, finishing 3.899 seconds ahead of his Mercedes team-mate Lewis Hamilton. Ferrari's Sebastian Vettel was third, 19.330 seconds back.

Bottas also set the fastest lap. It was his last win until the 2019 Australian Grand Prix.

The race was Felipe Massa's last in F1. He finished 10th for Williams, taking the final point.

Massa had planned to retire a year earlier. When Nico Rosberg quit suddenly and Bottas moved from Williams to Mercedes, he stayed on for one more season.

The Brazilian made 269 starts and won 11 races, driving for Ferrari from 2006 to 2013 and Williams from 2014. In 2008 he lost the title to Hamilton by one point.

## 2023: Verstappen ends a record year

Max Verstappen won the 2023 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix from pole, 17.993 seconds ahead of Ferrari's Charles Leclerc. George Russell was third for Mercedes.

It was Verstappen's 19th win from 22 races, a record for one season. He also became the first driver to lead more than 1,000 laps in a single season.

Sergio Perez crossed the line second but dropped to fourth after a five-second penalty for colliding with McLaren's Lando Norris. Russell's podium helped Mercedes beat Ferrari to second in the constructors' championship by three points.

The race was the last for the AlphaTauri name, before the team became RB, and the last for Alfa Romeo. It was also the final race in charge for Haas' Guenther Steiner and AlphaTauri's Franz Tost.
