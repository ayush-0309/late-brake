Two title rivals, one lapped car and a gap that opened at more than 300 kilometres an hour. Mika Häkkinen's pass on Michael Schumacher at Spa in 2000 still comes up whenever fans name F1's greatest overtakes.

## A wet start and a spin

The Belgian Grand Prix on 27 August 2000 was round 13 of 17. Häkkinen took pole for McLaren with a lap of 1:50.646, and Jarno Trulli's Jordan joined him on the front row. Williams rookie Jenson Button qualified third.

Rain had stopped before the start, but standing water left so much spray that the race began behind the safety car. Häkkinen led early, and Trulli and Button collided on lap 5.

On lap 13 Häkkinen touched a damp kerb at Stavelot and spun onto the grass. It cost him about ten seconds, and Schumacher's Ferrari went into the lead.

## Contact at Les Combes

As the track dried, Häkkinen reeled the Ferrari in. On lap 40 he tried to pass on the run to Les Combes, the corner at the top of the Kemmel straight.

Schumacher moved across late to defend. The cars touched, and Häkkinen's front wing was damaged.

## The pass

A lap later, Häkkinen was quicker through Eau Rouge and closed up again on the long climb. Ahead of them was Ricardo Zonta's BAR, about to be lapped.

Schumacher went one way around Zonta. Häkkinen went the other, took the inside line into Les Combes and came out in the lead. Zonta said later that he had seen the Ferrari in his mirrors but had no idea the McLaren was there.

Häkkinen held on for the last three laps and won by 1.104 seconds. Ralf Schumacher finished third for Williams.

It was Häkkinen's fourth win of the season and the 18th of his career.

## Words in parc fermé

After the race, Häkkinen went to Schumacher in parc fermé and told him not to defend like that at such speed again. Schumacher later praised the overtake.

The win put Häkkinen six points clear, on 74 to Schumacher's 68, with four races left.

Schumacher won all four remaining races, clinched the title at Suzuka on 8 October and became Ferrari's first drivers' champion in 21 years.

## Race it on Late Brake

Spa-Francorchamps is one of the six real circuits in Late Brake. Drive up through Eau Rouge and along the Kemmel straight to Les Combes, where the braking boards count you down, then race the AI rivals and look for a gap of your own.
