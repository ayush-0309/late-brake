The Las Vegas Grand Prix starts at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November, on the street circuit along the Las Vegas Strip.

The race runs at 8pm on Saturday night in Las Vegas, which is 13 and a half hours behind India, so every session falls in the Indian morning. It is a standard weekend with no sprint race.

## Full schedule in India time

- **Practice 1:** Friday 20 November, 6:00am to 7:00am
- **Practice 2:** Friday 20 November, 9:30am to 10:30am
- **Practice 3:** Saturday 21 November, 6:00am to 7:00am
- **Qualifying:** Saturday 21 November, 9:30am to 10:30am
- **Race:** Sunday 22 November, 9:30am

No session starts after midnight in India this time. Practice and qualifying run on Thursday and Friday evenings in Las Vegas, which is already Friday and Saturday morning in India.

## What's at stake

Kimi Antonelli and Mercedes have already won the drivers' and constructors' titles, so Las Vegas is about the places behind them. It is the first of the last three races, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

Max Verstappen is second in the drivers' standings on 279 points. George Russell has 262, Lewis Hamilton 261, Charles Leclerc 249 and Lando Norris 246, so five drivers are within 33 points of each other.

## The circuit

The Grand Prix runs over 50 laps of the 6.201 kilometre Las Vegas Strip Circuit, a distance of 309.958 kilometres, with a two-hour limit. Verstappen holds the lap record, a 1:33.365 from last year's race, which he won.

## Race it on Late Brake

Las Vegas isn't in Late Brake, but six real circuits are: Spielberg, Monza, Silverstone, Spa-Francorchamps, Monaco and Interlagos. Try a lap of Monaco's streets with the cockpit camera before the cars head out on the Strip, and see how close you can get to the gold medal time.
