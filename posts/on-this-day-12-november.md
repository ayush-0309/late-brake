A record winning margin in Adelaide and two Interlagos victories: here is what happened in F1 on 12 November.

## 1995: Hill wins the last Adelaide race by two laps

Damon Hill won the 1995 Australian Grand Prix for Williams-Renault from pole position, finishing two full laps ahead of everyone else.

Only eight cars were classified. Ligier's Olivier Panis was second and Footwork's Gianni Morbidelli third, both two laps down.

The weekend began badly. Mika Häkkinen crashed his McLaren at Brewery Bend after a puncture in Friday qualifying, fractured his skull and did not race.

In the race, David Coulthard crashed into the pit wall on his way into the pit lane, and Michael Schumacher and Jean Alesi collided and retired.

It was the last F1 race on the Adelaide streets. The Australian Grand Prix moved to Melbourne in 1996.

## 2017: Vettel wins at Interlagos as Hamilton storms through

Sebastian Vettel won the Brazilian Grand Prix for Ferrari, 2.762 seconds ahead of Mercedes' Valtteri Bottas, with Kimi Räikkönen third.

Vettel started second and passed polesitter Bottas into the first corner, then controlled the race from the front.

The drive of the day came from Lewis Hamilton, who had already won the title in Mexico two weeks earlier. He crashed in the first part of qualifying, started from the pit lane after his team changed his engine and gearbox, and finished fourth, 5.468 seconds behind Vettel.

## 2022: Russell wins his first sprint

George Russell won the sprint at Interlagos for Mercedes, starting third and finishing 3.995 seconds ahead of Ferrari's Carlos Sainz.

The win put him on pole for Sunday's São Paulo Grand Prix, where he took his first Grand Prix victory the next day, with Hamilton second for a Mercedes one-two.
