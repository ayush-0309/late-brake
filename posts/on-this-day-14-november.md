A four-way title decider in Abu Dhabi and a comeback through a weekend of penalties: here is what happened in F1 on 14 November.

## 2010: Vettel wins the title from third in the standings

Four drivers went to the final race at Yas Marina with a chance of the title. Ferrari's Fernando Alonso led on 246 points, ahead of Red Bull's Mark Webber on 238, Sebastian Vettel on 231 and McLaren's Lewis Hamilton on 222.

Vettel started from pole and won the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix by 10.162 seconds from Hamilton.

Alonso and Webber both made early pit stops, then spent much of the race stuck behind Vitaly Petrov's Renault. Alonso finished seventh and Webber eighth.

Vettel took the title by four points, 256 to Alonso's 252. At 23 years and 134 days, he became the youngest champion in the sport's history, beating Hamilton's 2008 record.

## 2021: Hamilton wins from tenth at Interlagos

Hamilton was fastest in qualifying for the São Paulo Grand Prix, but was disqualified because his rear wing's DRS opening was larger than the 85 mm the rules allow. DRS is the flap on the rear wing that opens to cut drag on the straights.

He started the sprint from 20th and finished fifth. A five-place grid penalty for using an extra engine then put him tenth on the grid for Sunday's race.

He passed his way to the front and won by 10.496 seconds from Red Bull's Max Verstappen, with his Mercedes team-mate Valtteri Bottas third.

The win cut Verstappen's championship lead from 19 points to 14, with three races left.
