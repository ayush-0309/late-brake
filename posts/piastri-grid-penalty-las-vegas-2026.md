Oscar Piastri will start this weekend's F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix 10 places lower than he qualifies, after McLaren confirmed he needs a new engine.

The 25-year-old has already used the four internal combustion engines each driver is allowed for the season, so a fifth one brings an automatic grid penalty.

## Why Piastri needs a fifth engine

McLaren said in its race preview that Piastri will take a new internal combustion engine, the petrol part of the Mercedes power unit, after a reliability issue.

It is the first time he has gone over the limit this year, which is why the drop is 10 places rather than a bigger one.

The team believes he can still fight back on the 6.201 kilometre street circuit, saying he "has a good chance to recover positions with overtaking".

## A new turbo as well

Piastri's car will also get Mercedes' upgraded turbocharger, which the engine maker earned under ADUO, F1's system that lets engine makers who fall behind make extra upgrades. McLaren expects the gain in performance to be small.

Champion Kimi Antonelli will run the same turbo at Mercedes. His team-mate George Russell will not, because he is also at his engine limit and the new part would cost him grid places.

## What happens next

Qualifying starts at 9:30am India time on Saturday 21 November, and the race at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November. The race runs at 8pm on Saturday night in Las Vegas, which is already Sunday morning in India. It is the first of the last three races of 2026, before Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
