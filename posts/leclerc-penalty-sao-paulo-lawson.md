Ferrari's Charles Leclerc was given a five-second time penalty after the F1 São Paulo Grand Prix for colliding with Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson.

The penalty drops Leclerc from seventh to eighth in the final results and moves Lawson up to seventh. It costs Leclerc two points.

## Contact at Junção

The pair were fighting over seventh place on lap 69 of 71 when they touched at Junção, Turn 12, the corner before the long run up to the finish line.

Both cars carried on, and Leclerc completed the pass soon afterwards. The stewards ruled that Lawson had the right to the corner and that the contact "clearly disrupted" his car.

They said the normal penalty would have been 10 seconds, but halved it because of the wet track and because the contact was minor.

Leclerc had started 14th after a grid penalty for new engine parts.

## Two unsafe releases

Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli was also given five seconds for an unsafe release from his final pit stop, into the path of Franco Colapinto's Alpine. He finished more than 20 seconds ahead of Oscar Piastri, so he keeps fifth.

Haas driver Oliver Bearman received the same penalty for an unsafe release. It does not change his 17th place.

During the race, Audi's Nico Hülkenberg was given a 10-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage at Turn 1. He still finished 10th and scored the final point.

## What happens next

Lando Norris won the race ahead of Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar. The next round is the Las Vegas Grand Prix, which starts at 9:30am India time on Sunday 22 November.
