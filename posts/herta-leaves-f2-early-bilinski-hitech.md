Colton Herta has ended his Formula 2 season early, leaving Hitech by mutual agreement with two rounds still to run.

Roman Bilinski takes his place for the final weekends in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, which run alongside the last two F1 races of the year.

## A hard first season

Herta, a 26-year-old American and nine-time IndyCar race winner, moved to European single-seaters this year to earn the superlicence points he needed to race in F1.

He sits 19th in the F2 standings with 26 points from 12 rounds. His best result was fifth in Barcelona, after starting third on the reversed grid used for F2's shorter sprint race.

## The F1 seat that went to Bottas

Herta is Cadillac's F1 reserve driver. He made six Friday practice appearances for the team this season, and they helped him secure his superlicence.

Days after he earned it, Cadillac confirmed Valtteri Bottas alongside Sergio Perez for 2027, which left Herta without a race seat. His plans for next year have not been announced.

## Bilinski arrives early

Bilinski, a 22-year-old Polish-Briton, was already due to drive for Hitech in 2027. He is 16th in the standings in his rookie F2 season, with 36 points for DAMS.

Hitech's Jan Sumann said the team was "delighted to welcome Roman to Hitech" for the last two rounds.

F2 leader Rafael Camara will also miss both rounds while he races for Haas in F1. The Qatar Grand Prix itself starts at 9:30pm India time on Sunday 29 November.
