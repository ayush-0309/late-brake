Two title deciders, ten years apart, both settled on 23 November.

## 2014: Hamilton wins the double-points finale

The 2014 season ended at Yas Marina with the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, the first and only F1 race to award double points.

Lewis Hamilton arrived with 334 points to Nico Rosberg's 317. With double points, he needed a top-two finish to be sure of the title, where sixth would have been enough under normal scoring.

Rosberg took pole, 0.386 seconds faster than Hamilton. In the race, his car lost power when its energy recovery system failed, and he dropped down the field to finish a lap behind.

Hamilton won the race by 2.576 seconds from Williams' Felipe Massa, with Valtteri Bottas third. It gave Hamilton his second world title, on 384 points to Rosberg's 317.

Williams' double podium earned the team 66 points, still a team record for a single race. The race was also Caterham's last: the team had returned after a crowdfunding campaign, and Will Stevens made his F1 debut.

## 2024: Russell wins as Verstappen takes title number four

Ten years later, George Russell won the Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole, leading Hamilton home for a Mercedes one-two. Carlos Sainz was third for Ferrari.

It was Mercedes' 60th one-two finish and their first since the 2022 São Paulo Grand Prix.

Max Verstappen came to Las Vegas 62 points ahead of Lando Norris. Fifth place was enough to clinch his fourth consecutive world title, and he left the race on 403 points to Norris's 340.
