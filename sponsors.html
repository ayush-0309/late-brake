Put your brand on the track

Late Brake is a free formula racing game played in the browser, on desktop and phones. Every board, runoff logo and grandstand roof around its six circuits can carry your brand.

Where your brand goes

Trackside boards

12-metre boards behind the barriers, grouped in braking zones and along the main straight, where players look hardest.

Per circuit or everywhere

Runoff logos

Your logo painted large on the tarmac runoff on the outside of corners. Hard to miss when a player runs wide.

Per corner

Grandstand and pit roofs

The biggest canvases on the circuit: grandstand roofs and the pit building, seen from above on every lap.

Per circuit

Circuit takeover

Every slot on one circuit in your colours, from the boards to the roofs. A natural fit for an event or launch.

Exclusive

Sponsored challenge

A weekly time challenge under your name, with your branding round the circuit and an optional prize for the fastest lap.
