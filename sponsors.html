Questions

Good to know

Do the ads interrupt the game?

No. Trackside slots are part of the circuit, like the boards at a real race. They never cover the screen or pause a lap.

Can we change the artwork during a campaign?

Yes, as often as you like. A new creative goes live within a day.

Can we have a circuit to ourselves?

Yes: that's the circuit takeover. No other brand appears on that circuit while it runs.

How do we know it worked?

We share player and lap numbers for the circuits you're on, from our site analytics and the game's own lap records.

Is the game tied to a racing series?

No. Late Brake is an independent game and isn't affiliated with any racing series, team or circuit.
