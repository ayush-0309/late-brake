About Late Brake

Late Brake is a free racing game you play in your browser, on six real circuits at true scale. It's made and run by Ayush Garg in India.

The game

Spielberg, Monza, Silverstone, Spa-Francorchamps, Monaco and Interlagos, each built from the real circuit's outline. Set lap times, earn bronze, silver and gold medals and race ghost laps. It's free to play, with no install, and new car colours unlock as you earn medals. You can read about each place on the circuit pages.

The blog

The blog covers Grand Prix racing: the latest news, race weekend times in India, history, fun facts and what happened on this day in past seasons. Four new posts go up every day between 9am and 6pm India time.

How posts are written

  • Posts are researched and written with the help of AI, from established motorsport news outlets, official announcements and published records. Every post links its sources at the end.
  • Facts are checked against those sources before a post goes up. Anything that can't be confirmed is left out, and we don't publish rumours about people.
  • Every photo comes from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence and is credited under the picture.
  • Ayush Garg is responsible for everything published here.

Corrections

If something in a post is wrong, email officialayushgarg@gmail.com with the post's link and what needs fixing, and we'll correct it.

Independence

Late Brake is independent. It isn't connected to, sponsored by or endorsed by any racing series, team, driver or circuit. Their names appear on the blog only to report news and history.
