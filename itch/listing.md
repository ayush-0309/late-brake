# Late Brake on itch.io

Everything to paste into the itch.io project page, field by field, plus the files to upload from this
folder. The game itself stays on latebrake.in: the itch.io page runs a small launcher (`late-brake-itch.zip`, zipped
from `launcher/`)
whose button opens the game there in a new tab, so sign-in, saved laps and car colours all work as normal.
This folder isn't part of the website (see `.vercelignore`).

The listing is part of the game, so like the game it never uses the championship's trademarked names.

## Description

Paste this into the Description box (it keeps its headings and lists).

---

**Late Brake** is a racing game on six real circuits, each built from the real track's outline at true scale:
Monza, Spa-Francorchamps, Silverstone, Monaco, Interlagos and Spielberg.

Brake as late as you dare, hit the apex and chase the perfect lap.

### What's in it

- Time trials with sector times, a live delta and your own ghost lap
- Races against computer drivers at three difficulty levels
- Online races for up to four players, leaderboards and a daily challenge
- Bronze, silver and gold medals on every circuit, which unlock new car colours
- Chase, cockpit and top-down cameras
- Real race-engine sound
- Keyboard, gamepad, or on-screen pedals on phones and tablets

### How to play

Press **Play** and the game opens on [latebrake.in](https://www.latebrake.in/) in a new tab. It's free, with no
download and no install. Play as a guest, or sign in with Google to keep your laps and medals on any device.

- **↑ / W** throttle, **↓ / S** brake
- **← → / A D** steer
- **T** reset to track, **R** restart, **Esc** pause
- **C** camera, **L** racing line, **G** ghost, **M** sound

### The circuits

Each circuit has its own page with its history, its corners and how to be fast there:
[latebrake.in/circuits](https://www.latebrake.in/circuits/)
