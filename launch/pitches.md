# Pitch emails: race-week bloggers and the press

These are the emails most likely to earn links from real sites, which is what raises the domain rating. Send them
yourself from officialayushgarg@gmail.com (the address on the site), one at a time, each one changed to fit the
person. A mass mail with only the name swapped gets ignored.

Rules that keep it working:

- Ask for nothing in return and never pay for a link. If someone asks for money, either decline or make sure the
  link is marked sponsored; a paid link that isn't can get the site penalised by Google.
- One follow-up at most (the last section), then leave it.
- No trademarked championship names (F1, Formula 1, Red Bull Ring). "Grand Prix", "the race at Monza" and the town
  names are fine.
- Keep a row per person in the tracker at the end, so nobody gets mailed twice.

## 1. Race-week pitch to motorsport bloggers

Send it 4 to 7 days before a race at one of the six circuits, to people who write race previews: blogs, Substack
newsletters, fan sites and YouTube channels (for those, ask for a line in the video description).

**Finding them:** search for these, with the circuit's name in place of [circuit]:

- `[circuit] race preview`
- `[circuit] preview substack`
- `[circuit] track guide corners`
- `motorsport newsletter race preview`

Skip big news sites at first; smaller blogs with a named writer reply more often. Indian motorsport blogs are
worth including, since you can mention being an Indian developer.

**Subject:** `Try [circuit] before Sunday`

> Hi [first name],
>

> liked or agreed with.]
>

> from the real track's outline at true scale. [The circuit's line from the list below.]
>
> If it's useful for your readers, the circuit's page has the map, the corners and a free race on it:
> [circuit page link]
>
> It might fit as a "try the track yourself" line in your next preview. Either way, thanks for the writing.
>
> Ayush Garg
> Late Brake, https://www.latebrake.in/

`free browser games roundup`, `free games of the week`, `best free racing games browser` and
`indie game newsletter`, and write to the person whose name is on the article.

Attach `itch/screenshot-1.jpg` and `itch/screenshot-2.jpg` (or a short clip), so they can see it without clicking.
