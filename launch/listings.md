# Listings: IndieDB, Game Jolt, Product Hunt

Pages you create and control, each with a link to latebrake.in. Do them before the launch posts
(`posts.md`), so anyone who searches for the game finds them. Publish the itch.io page first if it isn't public
yet (`itch/listing.md`).

Like the game and the itch.io page, these never use the championship's trademarked names (F1, Formula 1, Red Bull
Ring). Say "Grand Prix racing" or name the town. The pictures are the ones in `itch/`: `cover.png` and
`screenshot-1.jpg` to `screenshot-4.jpg`.

Field names below are what each site showed when this was written; if a form looks different, put the same text in
the closest field.

**Description:**

> Late Brake is a top-down racing game on six real circuits, each drawn from the real track's outline at true
> scale: Monza, Spa-Francorchamps, Silverstone, Monaco, Interlagos and Spielberg.
>
> Set a lap in time trial and race your own ghost, with sector times and a live delta on screen. Race up to three
> AI drivers at easy, medium or hard. Open a room and race up to three friends online. Every circuit has bronze,
> silver and gold medal times to beat, a leaderboard, and a daily challenge with its own board.
>
> The track limits work as they do in Grand Prix racing: put all four wheels past the white line and the lap is
> deleted.
>
> It runs in the browser on a computer, phone or tablet, with keyboard, gamepad or on-screen pedals. Time trials
> need no account. Sign in with Google to race AI drivers and other players, play the daily challenge and put your
> laps on the leaderboards.
>
> Made by one developer in India. Play it at https://www.latebrake.in/

Launch on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, at 12:01 am Pacific time: the day's ranking starts then. In India
that's 1:31 pm from November to mid March and 12:31 pm the rest of the year. Be around for the first few hours to
answer comments.

| Field | Value |

| Name | `Late Brake` |
| Tagline (60 characters at most) | `Race six real circuits, free in your browser` |
| Link | `https://www.latebrake.in/` |
| Topics | Games, Web App, Indie Games |
| Pricing | Free |
| Thumbnail | `icon-512.png` |
| Gallery | `cover.png`, then the four screenshots. A 30 second screen recording of a lap at Monaco in the chase camera does best as the first item |

**Description (260 characters at most):**

> A top-down racing game on six real circuits at true scale. Race your ghost in time trials, take on AI drivers or
> up to three friends online, and chase bronze, silver and gold medals. Free, plays on phones, keyboard or gamepad.

**First comment (post it as the maker as soon as the launch goes live):**

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm Ayush, and I made Late Brake on my own.
>
> I wanted a racing game I could open in a browser tab and be on a real circuit a few seconds later. So the six
> circuits are built from the real tracks' outlines at full scale, and the game is all about braking points:
> there's a racing line you can switch on (green for throttle, yellow for lift, red for brake) and braking boards
> before every big stop.
>
> Things to try:
> - Time trial at Monaco in the cockpit camera, with your ghost on
> - The daily challenge, which has its own leaderboard
> - Click any time on a leaderboard to race that driver's ghost
>
> Track limits are strict, as in real Grand Prix racing: all four wheels over the line and the lap is gone.
>
> It's free with no ads on latebrake.in. I'd love to hear which circuit you'd like added next, and anything that
> felt wrong in the handling.
