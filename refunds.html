Refund policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Late Brake is free to play. The only things you can buy are car colours, and a colour is yours the moment you pay, so all sales are final.

No refunds for a change of mind

We don't refund a colour because you changed your mind, stopped playing or prefer another one. A colour unlocks straight away, so when you buy one you agree to get it immediately and give up any right to cancel the purchase.

When we put it right

If something went wrong on our side, we fix it, at any time:

  • Charged twice for the same colour: we refund the extra payment.
  • Paid, but the colour never appeared in your Garage: we add it to your account, or refund you if we can't.

Nothing here takes away a right to a refund that the law gives you and that can't be given up.

How to ask

Email officialayushgarg@gmail.com with:

  • the email address you paid with (it's on your receipt);
  • which colour you bought, and roughly when;
  • the payment ID from your receipt, if you have it.

We aim to reply within a few days.

What happens next

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, so a refund is issued through Dodo to the card, UPI account or other method you paid with, in full. If you paid in a currency other than Indian rupees, it's refunded in that currency at the exchange rate on the day, so the amount can differ slightly from what you paid. It usually shows up within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. Refunding an extra payment doesn't touch the colour you kept; if a refund covers your only payment for a colour, it's taken off your account.

Before disputing a charge

Please email us first. We can usually sort it out much faster than a dispute with your bank.
