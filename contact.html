Contact

Late Brake is made and run by Ayush Garg in India. One email address covers everything.

officialayushgarg@gmail.com

We aim to reply within a few days.

Write to us about

  • The game: bugs, ideas, or a driver name or lap that shouldn't be on the leaderboards. Tell us your driver name, your browser and device, and what happened.
  • A purchase or refund: include the email address you paid with and the payment ID from your receipt. See the refund policy.
  • Your data: to see, correct or delete it, or to raise a grievance. See the privacy policy.
  • Advertising: trackside boards, runoff logos and more. See Advertise on Late Brake.

Payments

Car colours are sold through Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, so your receipt comes from Dodo. For anything about a purchase, email us first; you can also reach Dodo Payments through the links in your receipt.
